About

A software practice with an operator's habits.

MK313 builds production software and applied AI from Islamabad — and runs the operations that keep those systems honest.

Our story

Built from the inside of a working business.

MK313 began as a software and IT practice in Islamabad, led by founder Ali Jamal. We build practical operational systems — hiring workflows, rates and CRM tooling, invoicing, food-safety reporting — and the applied AI that supports them, always with human review where outcomes matter.

Alongside the software work, we operate StayConnect guest housing and MK Kitchen food service. Those businesses are not our marketing pitch; they are lived operational context. Running guest stays, staffing, delivery, food preparation and reporting gives us a concrete sense of where digital tools help — and where people still need to decide.

We keep claims tight. We describe systems we build and operate, without inventing scale, clients or maturity we cannot show publicly.

Mission

To design and deliver software and applied AI that support real operational work — intake, review, records and day-to-day workflows — with honesty about what is automated and what stays human-reviewed.

Vision

To be known in our markets for useful operational systems: clear interfaces, durable data flows, and AI assistance staff can trust because people remain accountable for outcomes.

What we believe

Transparent status. Reviewable records. No theatre.

Software should reduce friction in operations without pretending every decision can be automated. We prefer deployments that fit how teams actually work.
