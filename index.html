About Us

MK313 began as a software & IT solutions firm in Islamabad. Led by founder Ali Jamal, we’ve evolved into a multifaceted business bringing fresh, healthy juices and foods to our community.

We stand at the intersection of food and technology—driven by creativity, quality, and customer-centric values.
