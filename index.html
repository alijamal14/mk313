How we work

Start from the workflow, not the wireframe.

01

Understand the operation

Who does what today, where records break, and which decisions have to stay with a person. We would rather map the real process than assume a tidy one.

02

Build the smallest durable system

Something a team can run daily: clear status, reviewable records, and data flows that hold up under edge cases and unreliable connectivity.

03

Stay with it after launch

Changes come from what breaks and what staff actually ask for. Software that nobody maintains stops matching the operation within months.

Applied AI

Assistance that a person still signs off.

No autonomy theatre. Intake, extraction, retrieval and workflow support that staff can check — with people accountable wherever the outcome matters.

  • AI intake & structured extraction

    Parse inbound applications, documents or forms into structured fields for downstream review.

  • Human-reviewed retrieval

    Retrieval-assisted answers and knowledge lookup with people in the loop before action is taken.

  • Workflow automation

    Route, classify and queue work so teams spend less time on repetitive triage.

  • Private & on-premise deployment

    Support for deployments that keep sensitive intake and models closer to your own infrastructure.

  • Operational analytics

    Practical reporting on throughput, exceptions and review outcomes — not vanity dashboards.
